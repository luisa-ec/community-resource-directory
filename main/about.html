Getting to know Waypoint

About Waypoint

Helping people discover community support and access useful local information more easily.

Our purpose

Making community support easier to find

Waypoint is a community resource directory designed to help local residents, young people and newcomers discover relevant support services in their communities.

Finding assistance can sometimes be difficult when information is scattered across different organisations and platforms.

Waypoint aims to make this process easier by bringing information about different community services together in one accessible directory.

Who is the directory for?

The directory is designed with the following users in mind:

  • Young people looking for support and opportunities.
  • Newcomers trying to discover local services.
  • Residents looking for community assistance.
  • Individuals searching for education, employment or housing support.

What you can find

Areas of community support

Waypoint organises resources into five main support categories.

Education

Learning opportunities, tutoring, study spaces and skills development.

Health

Community health services, counselling and wellbeing support.

Employment

Job search guidance, CV assistance, interview preparation and training.

Housing

Housing advice, rental assistance and shelter support.

Youth Support

Mentoring, youth programmes, clubs and community activities.

About this project

Waypoint is being developed as a web development internship project. Its purpose is to demonstrate how a simple digital directory can organise community information and make it easier to explore.

The current version is a prototype. All organisation names, addresses, phone numbers and service descriptions used in the demonstration are fictional.
